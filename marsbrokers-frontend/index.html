01 / PROTOCOL OVERVIEW

What MarsBrokers does

MarsBrokers launches its protocol token through Flap and routes a defined share of trading fees into tokenized-stock purchases. Every MarsBroker NFT is paired with an ERC-6551 account that receives the stock assigned to that broker identity

The current NFT owner controls the broker account and can claim accumulated stock assets to a personal wallet or sell the NFT with unclaimed TBA holdings and future fee benefits included

01FLAP FEESProtocol trading creates fee flow
→
02BUY STOCKRouter purchases the assigned asset
→
03CREDIT TBAStock enters the broker wallet
→
04CLAIM OR SELLOwner chooses how to realize value

Core protocol principles

  • Fees create broker benefits The fee router converts the configured protocol share into tokenized stocks
  • Every broker keeps one stock identity The NFT ID determines which stock receives its allocation
  • Benefits live with the token-bound account Purchased assets are routed to the broker's ERC-6551 wallet
  • Ownership controls the outcome The owner claims accumulated assets or sells the broker with unclaimed benefits included
OWNER OPTIONSKeep the NFT and claim accumulated stock assets, use the broker and its TBA as a protocol position, or sell the NFT together with the unclaimed benefits held by its account
02 / TOKEN-BOUND ACCOUNTS

ERC-6551 broker wallets

ERC-6551 gives an NFT a deterministic smart-account address without changing the NFT contract itself. A registry creates or resolves an account using the implementation contract, chain ID, NFT contract and token ID. The resulting token-bound account is controlled through ownership of the NFT

ACCOUNT INPUTimplementation + chainId + nftContract + tokenId + salt

What the broker wallet can hold

TOKENIZED STOCKS

The fixed stock purchased from the protocol fee allocation

MARSCOIN

The protocol currency routed through the broker reward rail

NATIVE ASSET

BNB used for supported account execution and gas routing

FEE RECEIPTS

On-chain records linking fee rounds, purchases and broker allocations

Control versus custody

ERC-6551 does not automatically distribute rewards and does not guarantee that assets remain inside the wallet. Those rules must be enforced by the MarsBrokers claim, escrow or vault contracts. The account implementation must also define how calls are authorized and whether execution is restricted while the NFT is escrowed

IMPORTANTA buyer must inspect the broker's current TBA balances and claim state. Owning an NFT controls its account; ERC-6551 alone does not promise a particular balance
03 / NFT MARKET

Trading a broker and its benefits

The marketplace lists MarsBroker NFTs, not detached wallet balances. When settlement transfers the NFT, ERC-6551 ownership resolution changes to the buyer. Assets already held by the token-bound account remain at that account address, while control passes to the new NFT owner

1

Seller approves the market Approval must be limited to the verified MarsBrokers collection and marketplace contract

2

Listing is indexed Price, seller, token ID, expiry and listing nonce are checked against on-chain state

3

Buyer reviews the dossier The UI displays the assigned stock, Marscoin rail, TBA address, current balances and already-claimed periods

4

Settlement transfers the NFT The market sends payment and transfers the NFT atomically. TBA control follows ownership

5

Indexer refreshes ownership Market and Claim Center update only after transaction confirmation

Required buyer protections

  • Listings must include a nonce and expire or invalidate after transfer
  • The UI must re-read owner, approval and listing state before signing
  • If advertised TBA balances are part of the sale, settlement needs escrow or snapshot rules to stop the seller moving them before purchase
  • Future fee benefits are calculated by the distributor contract rather than inferred from interface copy

Fee conversion and allocation

The Flap token produces protocol fees in the configured settlement asset. The Fee Router calculates the distributable share, buys approved tokenized stocks and allocates the purchased amounts across eligible broker TBAs according to the on-chain distribution rule

The asset registry maps each display symbol to its chain ID, token contract and decimals so the router always purchases by verified address rather than ticker text

05 / DISTRIBUTION

How Claim Center works

Claim Center discovers the MarsBrokers owned by the connected wallet, resolves each ERC-6551 account and reads the fee-funded stock balances accumulated for every broker

1

Connect wallet The app resolves the wallet and its owned MarsBrokers

2

Select a broker The broker ID identifies its ERC-6551 account and predetermined stock

3

Read fee benefits Claim Center loads stock purchases credited by the Fee Router

4

Choose the outcome Keep the assets in the TBA, claim them to the owner wallet or sell the NFT with the benefits included

5

Review and sign The owner reviews asset, amount, source TBA, destination and network

6

Confirm ownership state Balances and NFT ownership refresh from the confirmed transaction

Double-claim prevention

Stocks already deposited in the TBA can only leave through an owner-authorized account execution. Every unsettled fee round records consumed allocations by broker ID and round identifier so ownership changes cannot duplicate a distribution

Safe marketplace disclosure

Every listing dossier must distinguish between current TBA holdings, currently claimable assets and possible future distributions. These are different states. The production UI must timestamp indexed data and link to the TBA and relevant contracts on a block explorer

07 / SECURITY MODEL

Security and transaction boundaries

VERIFIED REGISTRY

All token, router and account addresses resolve from the protocol registry

EXPLICIT APPROVALS

The interface explains NFT approvals and token allowances before signatures

STATE REVALIDATION

Owner, fee round, claim and balance state are re-read immediately before execution

ROUTER LIMITS

Fee conversion uses approved stock assets, bounded slippage and allocation rules

CONTRACT AUTHORITY

Contracts remain authoritative for fee accounting, purchases, TBA credits and claims

TBA EXECUTION SAFETY

The account implementation uses replay protection, owner checks and safe call restrictions

Primary protocol risks

  • Seller removes TBA assets after listing but before settlement
  • Stale ownership or claim data creates a misleading purchase dossier
  • Unrestricted ERC-6551 execution allows malicious calls or token approvals
  • Claim accounting resets or can be replayed after an NFT transfer
  • Fake stock symbols point to unverified token contracts
  • Compromised interface routing replaces trusted contracts
SECURITY STANDARDFee Router, marketplace, account implementation, distributor, vault and options contracts use independent audits and continuous monitoring
